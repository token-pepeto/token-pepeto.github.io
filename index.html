Pepeto is presented by its project as a meme-coin ecosystem combining a token, exchange concept, cross-chain bridge and token-screening tools.

Which networks does the project mention?

The official site describes connections involving Ethereum, BNB Chain and Solana. Check current official documentation for supported networks and live functionality.
